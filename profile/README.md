### Philosophy

Most stock companion apps for BLE haptic hardware lock basic control behind mandatory sign-ups, trackers, and proprietary clouds. **Noctwave** takes the opposite approach: everything works locally out of the box, and connected features stay under your own roof.

- **Local-First by Default** — direct Bluetooth Low Energy control with no mandatory registration or internet connection.
- **Optional Self-Hosting** — accounts, profile sync, and remote control links are strictly opt-in and designed to run on your own server.
- **Zero Telemetry** — no ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party data collection.
- **Cross-Platform & Open** — native BLE transport on iOS and Android with an auditable protocol implementation.
